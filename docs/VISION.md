# CoderaxAI — My Personal Autonomous AI Operating System

## 1. What is Coderax?

Coderax, also known as CoderaxAI, is my private, personal, owner-controlled artificial intelligence operating system. It is designed to become my centralized digital environment for artificial intelligence, software engineering, autonomous execution, research, creativity, automation, project management, problem-solving, and technological invention.

Coderax is not simply a chatbot, a coding assistant, a website generator, or a traditional AI agent. Its objective is to combine the strongest capabilities of numerous existing artificial intelligence platforms into one comprehensive, interconnected system that I personally own, operate, configure, and control.

I want Coderax to incorporate and eventually expand upon the capabilities found across platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, Cursor, Windsurf, FreeBuff, OpenCode, Base44, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, CodeRabbit, Greptile, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Manus, and other advanced AI platforms.

Rather than requiring me to constantly switch between different applications, subscriptions, websites, terminals, and AI assistants, Coderax should provide a unified environment where I can access equivalent capabilities through one intelligent system.

My long-term vision is for Coderax to become my personal digital workforce, engineering organization, creative production studio, research laboratory, cloud computing interface, and universal invention platform.

## 2. Who is Coderax designed for?

Coderax is designed specifically for me.

It is not intended to be a commercial SaaS product, a business-to-business platform, a membership service, a public chatbot, or a subscription-based application.

There should be no requirement to support customers, public registrations, subscription plans, marketing funnels, payment processing, or commercial user management.

I am the owner, administrator, primary operator, and sole intended end user of Coderax.

Every architectural, functional, and design decision should prioritize my personal experience, productivity, privacy, security, independence, and ability to accomplish complex objectives.

Coderax should continuously evolve according to my requirements rather than being constrained by what would appeal to a general consumer market.

CyVox is a separate project that may eventually become a public-facing product. Coderax must remain independent, with its own private infrastructure, authentication, data, agent orchestration, and security boundaries.

## 3. Why I am building Coderax

I am a software engineer who works with software development, backend engineering, enterprise applications, APIs, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and automation.

I also maintain multiple personal software projects and frequently explore new technologies, development frameworks, AI models, business concepts, and engineering opportunities.

Using separate AI products for every activity creates unnecessary fragmentation. Different platforms have different capabilities, usage restrictions, memory systems, tools, interfaces, and pricing structures.

I am building Coderax to eliminate that fragmentation.

I want one intelligent system capable of understanding my projects, remembering my previous decisions, accessing my authorized tools, researching new information, developing software, managing engineering activities, creating digital products, and executing work on my behalf.

Instead of repeatedly explaining my requirements to different AI assistants, Coderax should maintain the necessary knowledge and context to understand what I am working on, why I am working on it, and what needs to happen next.

Coderax should become increasingly useful through accumulated knowledge, reusable workflows, project history, and feedback.

## 4. My relationship with Coderax

I want to interact with Coderax as if I have my own personal AI engineering organization and digital workforce.

I should be able to communicate with it naturally, whether I am asking a simple question, exploring an idea, requesting software development, troubleshooting a system, researching an opportunity, or assigning an entire project.

For example, I should be able to say:

- Build this application from my description.
- Investigate why my deployment failed and resolve the problem.
- Review all my GitHub repositories and identify outstanding engineering work.
- Create a new mobile application and its backend.
- Research the newest artificial intelligence models and determine which ones I can use for free.
- Find software engineering jobs matching my professional experience.
- Create a movie from an original story.
- Develop a playable video game.
- Research an invention and help me create a working prototype.
- Continue working on my existing projects without requiring me to repeat previous instructions.

Coderax should understand the objective, determine the required steps, select the appropriate tools and agents, execute authorized actions, validate the results, and provide evidence of what it accomplished.

I want to spend more time defining objectives and making meaningful decisions, and less time performing repetitive technical work.

## 5. Unified Chat and Work experience

Coderax should provide two interconnected primary experiences: Chat and Work.

### Chat mode

Chat is my general-purpose intelligent assistant environment.

I should be able to use Chat for conversations, explanations, brainstorming, programming questions, research, personal productivity, document analysis, technical troubleshooting, image understanding, mathematical reasoning, creative writing, and general knowledge.

Chat should support streaming responses, multiple AI models, file attachments, conversation history, persistent memory, tool usage, and contextual awareness.

It should provide an experience comparable to leading conversational AI products while remaining integrated with Coderax's native architecture.

### Work mode

Work is the execution-oriented environment where Coderax moves beyond answering questions and performs actual tasks.

In Work mode, Coderax should be capable of developing applications, modifying repositories, running tests, creating documents, managing authorized cloud infrastructure, operating browsers, investigating technical problems, coordinating agents, and completing complex multistep assignments.

Work should support long-running tasks, progress reporting, cancellation, retries, recovery, checkpoints, artifacts, and execution history.

I should be able to move from a discussion in Chat directly into execution in Work without losing context or starting a separate application.

Both modes should exist within one consistent interface, not as unrelated products or separate websites.

## 6. Coderax Native Personal Agent

The most important component of Coderax is its own native personal AI agent.

The Coderax Native Personal Agent must be an independent agent implemented and controlled by Coderax itself.

It must not simply be a renamed FreeBuff agent, an OpenCode wrapper, a Claude Code wrapper, or a frontend connected to another organization's agent orchestration system.

Coderax must own its core intelligence and execution architecture, including:

- Task interpretation and planning.
- Agent orchestration and delegation.
- Tool selection and execution.
- Context management.
- Persistent memory.
- Project understanding.
- Owner authorization.
- Permission enforcement.
- Workflow execution.
- Error recovery and retries.
- Task scheduling.
- Result verification.
- Execution logs and auditing.
- Cloud workspace coordination.
- Model selection and routing.

The native agent should operate according to the policies, preferences, permissions, and operating rules that I configure within Coderax.

I should be able to define what it can access, what it can modify, when it can act autonomously, and when my approval is required.

Although Coderax may use external AI models and infrastructure, its native orchestration, permissions, and decision-making framework must remain under my control. External services will still be subject to their applicable terms, technical limitations, and legal requirements.

## 7. Multiple independent AI agent options

Coderax should eventually provide several clearly distinguishable agent experiences.

The default option should always be the Coderax Native Personal Agent.

Additional optional agents may include FreeBuff, OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, and other authorized agent integrations.

These agents must not be silently substituted for one another.

I should always know which agent is executing my task, which provider is being used, what information is being shared, which permissions are active, and whether the execution is subject to external provider restrictions.

Third-party agents should remain optional and isolated from the native Coderax agent.

FreeBuff may also assist with developing Coderax's frontend and visual design. However, it must not become a mandatory dependency for Coderax's production runtime or own its core security, orchestration, memory, or execution infrastructure.

## 8. Intelligent AI model selection

Coderax should support multiple AI model providers rather than being permanently dependent on one provider.

I want access to the strongest legitimately available models that satisfy my requirements, particularly models with advanced reasoning, software engineering, tool calling, multimodal understanding, planning, and autonomous agent capabilities.

Potential model families include Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Kimi, Llama, Mistral, and other suitable models.

Coderax should maintain a model capability registry containing information about model availability, reasoning capabilities, coding performance, context windows, tool support, multimodal capabilities, reliability, usage limits, and costs.

It should intelligently select models according to the objective being performed.

A simple question may require a lightweight model, while a difficult architecture problem may require a stronger reasoning model.

Complex tasks may benefit from multiple models independently examining a problem, comparing findings, and verifying proposed solutions.

However, Coderax must never silently switch to a paid model or consume a paid API allowance without my explicit authorization.

Model identities and availability must be verified rather than fabricated.

## 9. Zero additional spending

One of my fundamental requirements is that Coderax must operate with zero additional spending.

I do not want Coderax to depend on paid AI inference, paid cloud computing, paid coding-agent subscriptions, or infrastructure that automatically generates charges.

Coderax should investigate legitimate free cloud-hosted AI models, authorized free API tiers, recurring free allowances, qualifying programs, and free infrastructure.

It should monitor usage limitations and identify when a provider changes its free-tier conditions.

If a free service becomes unavailable, Coderax should identify another verified free alternative when possible.

If no legitimate free alternative exists, Coderax should report the limitation rather than automatically purchasing resources, upgrading an account, or routing requests to a paid service.

The system must enforce cost controls throughout its architecture, not merely display estimated costs after resources have already been consumed.

## 10. Cloud-only execution

Coderax should perform its computational workloads in authorized cloud environments.

My personal computer should not be used as the primary compute infrastructure for Coderax.

I do not want AI inference servers, development containers, databases, autonomous agents, browser workloads, or heavy engineering processes consuming my personal computer's CPU, RAM, storage, or GPU resources.

My computer and mobile devices should primarily provide interfaces for accessing and controlling Coderax.

The intended infrastructure includes free cloud services and my DeploymentPlatformAI project, subject to verified availability, security, capacity, and zero-cost requirements.

The longer-term infrastructure direction is to use Cloudflare Pages for frontend hosting where appropriate, Oracle Always Free infrastructure for backend workloads, and DeploymentPlatformAI as a cloud execution and deployment platform.

Cloud execution must include isolated workspaces, resource limits, task cancellation, secure credentials, workload monitoring, and protections against unexpected charges.

Coderax must not silently fall back to local Windows, WSL, local Docker, or local AI inference when cloud execution is unavailable.

## 12. GitHub and repository management

It should also verify that successful deployments actually correspond to the intended source code revision.

A task should not be considered complete merely because a pull request was opened or a deployment was initiated.

I expect Coderax to take responsibility for investigating and resolving errors encountered during authorized work.

It should use appropriate retry limits and avoid repeating unsuccessful actions indefinitely.

If a problem cannot be resolved because of missing permissions, unavailable infrastructure, insufficient free resources, external restrictions, or another genuine blocker, Coderax should clearly explain the exact limitation and what remains incomplete.

It must never fabricate successful execution, passing tests, completed deployments, or verified functionality.
