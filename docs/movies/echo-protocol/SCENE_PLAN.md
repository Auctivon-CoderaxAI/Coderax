## Scene-to-shot method

Coderax plans coverage with a fixed six-beat grammar: establishing wide, approach,
reaction close-up, dialogue coverage, threshold reveal, final hold. The planner writes
camera, lens, lighting, action, and continuity requirements into every shot and refuses
to mark a shot rendered without a verified artifact.

Durations in the seed are planning estimates, not locked edits. Dialogue timing is
computed from the written line and can be overridden after a voice pass.
